## The Next Generation of Cognitive Robotics

By combining cutting-edge photonic intelligence with adaptive robotics, the research project EDISON (Effizientere flexible Produktionsprozesse durch Assistenzsysteme mit kontextbezogener Photonik-Intelligenz) is shaping the future of human-centered manufacturing. The initiative contributes directly to strengthening sustainable, resilient, and intelligent automation Made in Germany.

At its core, EDISON aims to transform industrial production by developing human-centered, adaptive, and trustworthy cognitive robotic systems. By integrating context-aware photonics and artificial intelligence, the project enables flexible assistance systems that can safely and efficiently collaborate with human operators in dynamic production environments.

## Strong Consortium Driving Innovation

The project brings together a powerful consortium of leading academic and industrial partners:

- Neuro-Information Technology NIT (OvG-Universität Magdeburg)
- MartinMechanic Friedrich Martin GmbH & Co. KG
- LUCAS instruments GmbH
- HEMA Formenbau + Kunststoffverarbeitung GmbH
- Zentrum für Bild- und Signalverarbeitung in Technik, Medizin und Umwelt e.V. (ZBS e.V.)
- Associated Partner: Pilz GmbH & Co. KG

## Milestones in Motion

On October 5, 2026, the project partners gathered at ZBS e.V. in Ilmenau for their annual project meeting to review progress and align on key technological developments toward building the next generation of cognitive robotics.
